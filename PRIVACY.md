# Privacy Policy for Whisper

**Last Updated: [15/09/26]**

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what data Whisper ("the Bot") collects, how we
use it, and your choices. We aim to collect as little as possible.

## 2. What We Collect

When you use the Bot, we may store:

- **Discord User ID** — a numeric identifier. Used to enforce rate limits,
  prevent abuse, and allow you to delete your own posts.
- **Discord Server (Guild) ID** — identifies which server a post came from.
- **Message Content** — the anonymous text you submit.
- **Timestamps** — when a post was made.
- **Channel ID** — where the post was made.

We do NOT collect your email, real name, IP address, payment info, or
message history outside of Bot commands.

## 3. How We Use It

- To display your anonymous post in the correct server
- To prevent spam and abuse
- To comply with legal obligations
- To improve the Bot's features

## 4. Anonymity

Your Discord User ID is stored separately and is never shown to other
members. However, anonymity is not absolute — we can technically link a
post to a user ID if required for abuse investigations or by law.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your data. We do NOT share it with advertisers.

We may share data only:

- With Discord, if required by their Terms or API rules
- With law enforcement, if legally required
- To prevent serious harm or illegal activity

## 6. Data Retention

Anonymous posts are retained for as long as the Bot is in the server, or
until deleted. User IDs are retained for abuse prevention. You may request
deletion of your data by contacting us.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your region (e.g., GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA in California),
you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. To make a
request, contact us at [YOUR EMAIL].

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended for users under 13 (or the minimum age in your
country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored data, but no system is
100% secure. Use the Bot at your own risk.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of the Bot means you accept the
changes.
